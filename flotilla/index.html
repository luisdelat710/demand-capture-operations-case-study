Business rules

How the figures are counted

  • Expected reports = Monday–Saturday dates in the month × active units; duplicate unit/date entries count once.
  • Missing = expected − reported. This illustrative rule flags more than three missing unit-days for review.
  • Fuel share = fleet fuel spend ÷ monthly sales × 100. Unit efficiency = odometer difference ÷ logged liters.
