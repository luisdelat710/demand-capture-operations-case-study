Business rules

What drives the suggestions

  • External service routes to an external carrier; own-fleet requests up to and including 1,230 kg suggest the light truck, above that the heavy truck.
  • A possible combined trip requires another light-truck order on the same date and zone, within 120 minutes, with combined weight above 1,200 kg.
  • The combined-trip prompt is a review cue, not automatic reassignment or routing optimization.
Portfolio context

Built for a real operations need

The original AI-assisted application included order capture, invoice attachment, screenshot-assisted extraction, calendar views and exports. This public recreation isolates the decision rules with mock data. It has no login, database, upload or OCR connection.
