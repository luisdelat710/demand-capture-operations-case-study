01 · Problem

Informal demand was hard to analyze

Customer requests for products not immediately available were difficult to aggregate consistently, which reduced visibility into recurring demand and useful purchasing signals.

02 · My Role

Translate the operating problem into structure

I defined what information needed to be captured, the categories to use, the management views needed, and the rules for turning frontline inputs into usable records.

03 · Business Rules

Capture enough detail without slowing sales

The workflow records category, format, color, style, approximate square meters and salesperson. Controlled options are used where consistency matters; lightweight normalization handles common free-text variation.

04 · Solution

Capture + dashboard in one lightweight flow

The demo lets frontline users register a request and lets management review request count, approximate volume, top categories, top formats and detailed records.

05 · Validation

Review outputs against the intended process

I used AI-assisted development to accelerate implementation, then reviewed whether the fields, calculations, categories and management views matched the operating need. I do not present the project as independent software engineering.

06 · What I Learned

Data quality starts at the point of capture

Free-text inputs quickly create duplicate categories and inconsistent labels. A better design balances controlled inputs with enough flexibility for frontline users to record what actually happened.
